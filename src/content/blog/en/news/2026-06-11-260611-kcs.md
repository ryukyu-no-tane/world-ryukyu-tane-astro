*Kina Career Education School Project*

![The community meeting for the Kina Career Education School Project](https://news.ryukyu-tane.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/77720_0.jpg)

On June 11, 2026, we held our first community meeting of the year at the Kina Community Center.

Many people joined the meeting. They came from schools, the local government, the council of social welfare, and the chamber of commerce. We thank everyone who gave their time to support local children.

## Why We Held the Meeting

We looked back at the work from the last school year. We also shared our plan for the new year. Our goal is to build a safer and better-organized network for children.

![A slide about the goal of the meeting](https://news.ryukyu-tane.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/スクリーンショット-2026-06-12-7.18.46.png)

The report showed some important changes. Some students joined workplace activities and later returned to school. Some also became able to greet other people.

We also found problems. The project depended too much on the kindness of a few people. We also needed better rules for safety, law, and management.

## From Kindness to a Clear System

This year, we will work on three main goals.

1. **The whole community will support children.**
   Children need to meet many different adults, not only their parents and teachers. The “Yomitan Network” will create these chances. It will also stop too much work or responsibility from falling on one person.

   ![A slide about community support](https://news.ryukyu-tane.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/スクリーンショット-2026-06-12-7.19.20.png)

2. **We will make children’s progress easier to see and share.**
   Children can earn points for actions such as going to school, joining a HOP class at the community center, or visiting places in the community. The points can help children see their own progress. We will also look at using school tablets to share information safely.

   ![A slide about showing progress and sharing information](https://news.ryukyu-tane.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/スクリーンショット-2026-06-12-7.19.22.png)

3. **We will protect children with clear rules.**
   We will use an agreement signed by four groups: KCS, the child’s parent or guardian, the school, and the workplace or group that receives the child. We will follow Japan’s labor and child welfare laws. We also want to support young people who do not enter high school or who leave school before graduation.

   ![A slide about rules and safety](https://news.ryukyu-tane.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/スクリーンショット-2026-06-12-7.19.24.png)

## The Yomitan Network

People at the meeting talked deeply about problems facing local children.

They agreed that one company or one person should not carry all the work. The whole Yomitan community should support children who want to try something new. The group also talked about safe places for children and learning how to manage risk.

## What Comes Next

The chair of the Kina Community Association shared this message: “When our community has a problem, the whole community should solve it. We want the Yomitan Network to support our children.”

We will respect a child’s own choice to join an activity. Schools, families, and local people will work together to give support in one connected system.
