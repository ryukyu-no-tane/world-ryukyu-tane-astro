On June 11, 2026, we had our first community meeting of the year at the Kina Community Center.

People from schools, the local government, the council of social welfare, and the chamber of commerce were at the meeting. We give our thanks to everyone who gave time for local children.

## Why We Had the Meeting

We had a look at the work from the last school year. We also had a plan for the new year. Our goal is a safer and better-organized network for children.

![A slide about the goal of the meeting](https://news.ryukyu-tane.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/スクリーンショット-2026-06-12-7.18.46.png)

The report had information about important changes. Some students had workplace activities and later went back to school. Some also got more confidence with greetings.

We also got a clear view of some problems. Too much of the project was on the kindness of a few people. We also had to get better rules for safety, law, and management.

## From Kindness to a Clear System

This year, we will have three main goals.

1. **The whole community will give support to children.**
   Children should have time with many different adults, not only their parents and teachers. The “Yomitan Network” will make these chances. It will also make sure that one person does not have all the work.

   ![A slide about community support](https://news.ryukyu-tane.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/スクリーンショット-2026-06-12-7.19.20.png)

2. **We will make children’s progress clear and give information safely.**
   Children can get points when they go to school, have a HOP class at the community center, or go to places in the community. The points can give each child a clear view of their progress. We may also have school tablets for safe information between people.

   ![A slide about progress and safe information](https://news.ryukyu-tane.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/スクリーンショット-2026-06-12-7.19.22.png)

3. **We will give children protection with clear rules.**
   We will have an agreement among four groups: KCS, the child’s parent or guardian, the school, and the workplace or group with the child. We will go by Japan’s labor and child welfare laws. We will also give support to young people who do not go into high school or who go out before graduation.

   ![A slide about rules and safety](https://news.ryukyu-tane.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/スクリーンショット-2026-06-12-7.19.24.png)

## The Yomitan Network

People at the meeting had a deep talk about problems for local children.

They had the same view: one company or one person should not have all the work. The whole Yomitan community should give support to children with a wish for something new. The group also had a talk about safe places for children and good ways to take care with risk.

## Next Steps

The chair of the Kina Community Association gave this message: “When our community has a problem, everyone should take part. The Yomitan Network should give support to our children.”

We will give respect to each child’s own choice about an activity. Schools, families, and local people will make one connected system for support.
