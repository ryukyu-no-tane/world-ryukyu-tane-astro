On May 23, 2026, Unna Junior High School had a talk for parents and guardians. Kaname Sadoyama was the speaker.

The theme was “Children and Their Strengths: A Sense of Competence and Multiple Intelligences.” Many parents and guardians were there. They had time together for their children’s growth and future.

## The Feeling “I Can Make It”

Sadoyama gave information about a **sense of competence**. It is the feeling, “I can make it.” This feeling can give a child power when a problem is hard.

He also gave information about Howard Gardner’s **theory of multiple intelligences**. In this theory, people can have different kinds of strength. For example, one child may be good with pictures. Another may be good at music, movement, language, or time with other people.

## What Parents Got from the Talk

Seventeen people gave answers in a questionnaire after the talk. Three ideas were very important to them.

- **Give time and attention to the child**
  First, have time for the child’s words. Do not give criticism first.
- **Give praise for effort and process**
  Do not have only the final result in mind. Give attention to the child’s effort and each step.
- **Get a view of different strengths**
  The eight kinds of intelligence gave parents a new view of their children’s strengths. These strengths can be in pictures, music, movement, language, and time with other people.

## New Ideas for PTA Activities

The talk gave parents new energy. They gave ideas for future PTA activities.

- **Food:** make katsu curry before school entrance exams and have more cooking activities
- **Sports and friendship:** have sports days and volleyball games, and have more time with people from the community
- **Making things:** make muumuu dresses and get a view of Okinawan culture
