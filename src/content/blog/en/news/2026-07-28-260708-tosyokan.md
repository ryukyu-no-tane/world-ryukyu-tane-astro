On July 8, 2026, the Chubu School Library Association had a meeting at the Yomitan Village Cultural Center. A member of Ryukyu no Tane was in the panel discussion.

The theme was “Hearts, Books, and School Libraries.” About 200 school librarians and teachers from central Okinawa were there. The Okinawa Times newspaper also had an article about the meeting.

![The panel discussion](https://news.ryukyu-tane.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/IMG_9163-1024x768.jpeg)

## Three People on the Panel

The panel had three people:

- Yosuke Moriyama, the PTA president at Onna Elementary School
- Naoko Henza, a childcare worker and writer
- a member of Ryukyu no Tane

Moriyama gives children small mysteries in book time. His message is that children can get answers in the library. He gave examples from Okinawan place names, family names, and word games. These examples give children the joy of “Why?” and the wish to go to books for answers.

Henza gave a story about a student who had difficulty with other people. The library was a safe and calm place for the student. The student made a novel and later had it at a book event with the name “literary flea market.” This story gives a picture of how a library can give children ways to have books and make their own work.

## AI and Books

Ryukyu no Tane gave two ideas about digital tools and libraries.

The first idea has **multiple intelligences theory**. In this theory, people can have different kinds of strength. For example, a child may be good at music, movement, pictures, or time with people. Adults can get a view of a child’s strengths and then give book ideas that have a link with the child’s interests.

The second idea has **AI and the Nippon Decimal Classification (NDC)**. NDC is the number system for groups of books in many Japanese libraries.

A child can first get book ideas from an AI tool, such as ChatGPT. The child can then get the NDC number for books about their interests. After that, the child goes to the library and goes to that shelf.

AI can give the child words for their interests. The library gives the child a real place for new ideas. Many people at the meeting had an interest in this idea for school.

## The Library as a Safe Place

A school library is not only a place for books. It can also be a safe place for children.

Some children have difficulty going to class or school. The library may give them a calm place with no worry about grades or tests. In this way, it can be like the school nurse’s room.

Japanese sentences often have no subject in the text. This can be difficult for children with Japanese as a new language. One family also gave an idea with the name “otoshibon.” At New Year, their child can get one book with no worry about its price.

## What People Got from the Meeting

Satsuki Arakaki, a librarian at Kitanakagusuku Elementary School, got a lot from the meeting. She has hope that libraries can make connections among children, teachers, and local people.

The meeting had one main message: libraries can make connections among children, adults, and their community.
